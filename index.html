Scanner rules & diagnostics

Signals use closed 1m candles, aligned 5m/15m context, adaptive momentum, RSI, candle confirmation, optional volume confirmation and ATR risk. A sideways 1m trend may qualify only when price holds the EMA21 in the higher-timeframe direction. Two closed candles are required. A single WAIT candle does not erase an active signal.
