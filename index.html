Scanner rules & diagnostics

V7 keeps every entry filter unchanged. It separates first-candle LONG/SHORT candidates from confirmed signals and HELD paper positions. WAIT reasons are assigned to one first blocker per coin, so category totals do not double-count. Bullish pullback / bearish breakdown rules have not been loosened or declared profitable; live-feed diagnostics are not a historical backtest.

This scanner uses a broad tracked-symbol list, not a verified complete Mudrex contract directory. Mudrex documentation describes public price klines and ticker streams; the authenticated Futures asset-list endpoint is not used here. Missing candles never create invented signals. Price updates can arrive around 1 second; signals use closed candles and confirmation to reduce flicker. Funding, open interest and live news are not included unless a reliable public feed is available. Manual paper analysis only; no orders are placed.
