Signal ka matlab

LONG = bullish setup mila; futures mein long direction. Ye profit ki guarantee nahi.

SHORT = bearish setup mila; futures mein short direction. Mudrex par instrument aur order rules khud verify karna.

WAIT = conditions poori nahi hui, data adhura/purana hai, ya setup unclear hai. WAIT ko zabardasti LONG/SHORT mein nahi badla jayega.

Signals closed candles par based hain. 1m, 5m aur 15m trend, RSI aur momentum use hote hain. Ye system abhi independently validated nahi hai. News/event analysis included nahi hai.
